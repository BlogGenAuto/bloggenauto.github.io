Frequently Asked Questions

How much weight can a 1-meter rail actually hold?

When properly installed into a solid wall using the provided fixings, a GearRail® can support up to 100kg. This is significantly more than any standard guitar wall mount no screws solution, making it safe for even the heaviest vintage gear or multi-instrument setups.

Will this work on plasterboard or drywall?

Yes, the rail can be fitted to plasterboard if you screw into the wooden battens/studs behind the board. For direct-to-plasterboard mounting without studs, we recommend using high-quality toggle bolts, though the weight capacity will be limited by the strength of the board itself.

Can I use this for things other than guitars?

Absolutely. The modular system is designed to be "universal." By heading to the custom collection, you can find hooks for everything from headphones and cables to heavy amplifiers or even mountain bikes, all on the same rail system.

Is a guitar wall mount no screws safe for nitrocellulose finishes?

You should always check the padding of the specific hooks you buy. GearHooks uses protective coatings, but for vintage nitrocellulose finishes, it is a good practice to ensure the contact points are compatible with your specific instrument's lacquer.

How hard is it to move the hooks once the rail is up?

It is incredibly easy. You simply loosen the wing nut or bolt on the hook, slide it to the new position on the rail, and tighten it back up. No new holes are required, which is the primary benefit over traditional single-mount hangers.
