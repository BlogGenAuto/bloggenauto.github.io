Frequently Asked Questions

What does a shave bar do?

A shave bar serves a dual purpose: it cleanses the skin of oils and impurities while providing a lubricating layer for a razor. By combining a face wash and a shaving cream, it simplifies the grooming process and ensures the skin is properly prepared for the blade, reducing the risk of irritation.

How to make a shave bar at home?

Making a basic shave bar at home involves a process of saponification using fats like coconut oil or cocoa butter combined with lye and water. Many DIY enthusiasts also add bentonite or Kaolin clay for slip. However, professional sticks like HARROMAN are formulated with specific pH-balanced ingredients and cooling menthol that are difficult to replicate in a home kitchen.

How long does a shave bar last?

On average, a 40g shave bar can last between 60 to 90 days with daily use. Because the formula is concentrated and the application is targeted, there is far less waste compared to aerosol foams where much of the product ends up washed down the drain before it ever touches your face.

How do you use a shaving bar?

Using a shaving bar is simple: wet your face with warm water, twist up the stick, and apply it directly to your beard area. Massage the product with your fingers to create a light, creamy lather. Shave as usual, then rinse with cool water to close the pores and enjoy the menthol cooling effect.
