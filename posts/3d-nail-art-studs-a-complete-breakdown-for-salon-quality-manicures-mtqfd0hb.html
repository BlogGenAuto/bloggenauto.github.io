Placement and Adhesion

Apply a small bead of builder gel or high-strength resin to the center of the stud. Use a wax picker tool—a staple in any nail art supplies near me toolkit—to precisely place the charm. Flash cure if using gel to lock it into position immediately.

Sealing the Edges

One common mistake is leaving gaps under the stud. Use a thin detail brush to apply top coat around the base of the decoration. This prevents hair and fabric from getting caught, which is a tip frequently shared in professional circles.
