Step-by-Step: The 10-Minute Modular Wall Rail Installation

Musicians have limited time to spend on tedious home renovations. The entire mounting process for a white guitar wall mount rail takes under 10 minutes from unpacking to hanging your first guitar.

Step 1: Position and Level

Hold the 1-metre rail against your desired wall location at your preferred operating height. Place a spirit level on top of the rail and use a pencil to mark the three pre-drilled fixing slots.

Step 2: Drill 3 Precision Holes

Using an appropriate masonry bit for brick walls or a standard wood bit for timber studs, drill the 3 marked spots. Insert the supplied heavy-duty nylon wall plugs flush into the holes.

Step 3: Secure Bolts and Slide Hooks

Drive the 3 hex-head bolts through the rail into the anchors using an Allen key. Pop the sleek black end caps into place, slide on your custom guitar hooks along the track, and lock them down. Your studio gear organizer is complete!

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install a white guitar wall mount rail into standard plasterboard walls?

Yes. When installing onto plasterboard or hollow partition walls, fix the rail directly into the vertical timber studs using the supplied hex-head bolts. If studs are spaced awkwardly, a basic timber batten easily bridges the gap to provide an ultra-secure foundation.

How many instruments fit onto a single 1-metre GearRail®?

Depending on whether you mount your instruments flat or angled, a single 1-metre rail comfortably supports between 3 and 5 full-sized electric or acoustic guitars. For larger collections, you can mount additional rails side-by-side seamlessly.

Will the hooks mark or discolor my instrument necks?

No. GearHooks® instrument prongs are engineered with non-reactive, heavy-duty protective sleeving designed to cushion headstocks and prevent finish damage, even under prolonged studio storage.

What makes cold rolled steel superior to aluminum tracks?

Cold rolled steel offers significantly higher tensile yield strength than aluminum. This ensures zero sagging or rail flex, maintaining a rigid 100kg capacity that easily withstands dynamic instrument handling.

Can I slide and rearrange hooks after mounting the rail?

Absolutely. That is the core advantage of a modular wall rail. You simply loosen the secure fixing bolt, reposition your hook anywhere along the 20+ mounting slots, and tighten it down again without drilling fresh holes.

Does the rail come with all necessary hardware?

Yes. Every GearRail® arrives with heavy-duty hex-head wall bolts, expanding nylon fixings suitable for brick, block, and stone walls, end caps, and full instructions to ensure a simple 10-minute setup.
