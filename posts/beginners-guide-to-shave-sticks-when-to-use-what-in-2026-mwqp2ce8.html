Step-by-Step Beginner Guide: How to Apply and Shave

Operating a twist-up shave stick requires slightly different technique than squirting canned foam into your palms:

  1. Prep With Warm Water: Thoroughly wet your face and neck with lukewarm water to soften tough facial hair fibers.
  2. Direct Surface Application: Twist up approximately a quarter-inch of the product. Rub the stick gently in circular motions across your stubble until a fine, creamy layer coats the entire shaving zone.
  3. Lather With Your Fingers: Wet your hands and massage the product against your grain for 20 seconds. Watch the kaolin clay and shea butter transform into a creamy, protective micro-lather.
  4. Shave With Short Strokes: Glide a sharp, clean razor along your beard grain without applying heavy downward pressure. Rinse the blade frequently under running water.
  5. Cool Rinse: Splash your face with cool water to rinse away residue and activate the refreshing menthol tingle.
