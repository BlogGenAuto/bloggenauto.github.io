FAQ: Mastering Your Guitar Wall Mount Sideways Setup

How to hang up a guitar sideways?

Hanging a guitar sideways requires a specialized rail system or angled hooks that support the instrument by the neck and body or at an offset angle. What is the hardest guitar style to play?

While subjective, many musicians consider classical or flamenco guitar to be the most technically demanding due to the intricate fingerstyle techniques and precision required. Regardless of the style you master, keeping your instruments organized on a guitar wall mount sideways ensures they are always within reach for those difficult practice sessions.

What is the guitar clamp thing called?

The device used to hold a guitar on a wall is typically called a guitar hook, hanger, or wall mount. In the context of performance, a "clamp thing" used on the neck to change the pitch of the strings is called a capo. For studio storage, professional-grade guitar wall mount sideways hooks provide the most secure grip while remaining easily adjustable along a mounting rail.

What does hand orientation mean in guitar?

Hand orientation refers to whether a guitar is designed for a right-handed or left-handed player, determined by which hand is used for fretting and which for picking/strumming. Right-handed players fret with the left hand and strum with the right, while left-handed players do the opposite. When setting up your guitar wall mount sideways, consider your dominant hand to ensure the most ergonomic access to your instruments, especially when they are stored in high-density configurations.
