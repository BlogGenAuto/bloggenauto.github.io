Frequently Asked Questions

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are highly recommended for guitars as they keep instruments off the floor where they are prone to impact damage. High-quality mounts that use silicone-coated prongs prevent finish reactions, and elevating the guitar allows for more consistent airflow around the instrument's body and neck.

How can I hang my guitar on the wall?

You can hang your guitar using a dedicated wall hook or a rail-based system like the GearHooks GTR. Simply locate the studs in your wall for maximum support, drill the required holes, and secure the mount. For larger collections, a guitar wall mount argos rail system is the most efficient way to manage weight and space.

What is the best way to hang guitars on the wall?

The best way is to use a modular rail system that allows for horizontal adjustment. This ensures you can space out acoustics and electrics perfectly. For the best security, always choose hooks with a protective coating and a slight upward angle or strap to prevent the instrument from sliding forward.

Is it better to hang a guitar or keep it in a case?

While cases offer maximum protection from humidity swings, hanging a guitar on the wall ensures you actually play it more often. If your room is climate-controlled, a wall mount is the superior choice for accessibility and display. For vintage instruments, a wall mount with silicone sleeves is safe and effective for long-term storage.
