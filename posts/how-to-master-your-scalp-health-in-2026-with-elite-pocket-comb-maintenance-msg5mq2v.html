Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I clean my pocket comb in 2026?

For optimal scalp health for men 2026, you should aim to clean your comb at least once a week. If you use heavy styling products like pomades or waxes daily, a quick mid-week rinse with warm water is highly recommended to prevent residue buildup.

Can I use alcohol to sanitize my oil-infused comb?

It is best to avoid high-percentage isopropyl alcohol, as it can degrade the oil-infused comb advantages and dry out the material. Stick to gentle soap or natural antibacterials like diluted tea tree oil to ensure your men's grooming tool lasts for years.

Why is an anti-static comb better than a standard one?

A standard plastic comb often generates a negative charge, causing hair to fly away and the cuticle to roughen. An anti-static comb benefits you by neutralizing this charge, resulting in a smoother appearance and less physical damage to the hair shaft during styling.
