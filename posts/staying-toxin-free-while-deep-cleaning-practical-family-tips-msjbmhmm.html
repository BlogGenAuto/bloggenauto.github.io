Frequently Asked Questions

How do you clean a steam cleaner?

To clean your clean steam cleaner, you should regularly descale the internal tank with a mixture of water and white vinegar to remove mineral buildup. This is especially important if you live in a hard water area. Ensure the device is unplugged and completely cool before rinsing the tank and wiping down the nozzles with a soft cloth.

What should you not clean with steam cleaner?

You should avoid using a steam cleaner on unsealed hardwood floors, delicate silks, or anything sensitive to high heat. Additionally, avoid cleaning cold glass windows in winter, as the sudden temperature change could cause the glass to crack. Always test a small, inconspicuous area before proceeding with a full clean.

Are steam cleaners actually good for cleaning?

Yes, steam cleaners are exceptionally effective because they use heat to break the bonds that hold dirt and bacteria to surfaces. Research from the World Health Organization suggests that heat is one of the most reliable methods for disinfecting surfaces. Steam penetrates microscopic cracks that traditional mops and wipes simply glide over.

Can I put Dawn dish soap in my steam cleaner?

No, you should never put soaps or detergents inside a clean steam cleaner tank. These machines are designed to work with pure water only; adding soap can clog the internal heating element and permanently damage the 1500W motor. If a surface requires extra soap, apply it directly to the surface first, then use the steam to blast it away.
