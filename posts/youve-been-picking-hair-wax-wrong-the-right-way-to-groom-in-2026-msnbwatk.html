How to Apply Styling Cream for a Professional Look

Achieving professional grooming at home is 10% product and 90% technique. To use a cream pomade correctly, start with slightly damp hair. Scoop a dime-sized amount and emulsify it between your palms until it disappears. This ensures you don't get "clumps" of product in one spot.

Work from Back to Front

The biggest mistake men make is applying product directly to the bangs first. This leads to a heavy, greasy forehead. Instead, start at the crown and work forward, finishing with the fringe. This creates a balanced, lightweight feel that lasts all day.

Finishing for the Natural Look

Once the product is distributed, use a comb or your fingers to define the shape. Because this is a water-soluble pomade for daily use, you can add a tiny bit of water to your hands later in the day to restyle if necessary. Solving the Scalp Buildup Problem

A major question in 2026 is: why is my hair wax causing buildup? Most traditional waxes use high concentrations of paraffin or petrolatum. These ingredients are not water-soluble, meaning they require aggressive clarifying shampoos to remove. Over time, this strips the scalp of natural oils, leading to dandruff and hair thinning.

By switching to a water-based formula, you ensure that the product rinses away with simple water or a gentle daily shampoo. This keeps the follicles clear and healthy. The FDA monitors cosmetic ingredients for safety, but it's up to the consumer to choose formulas that align with their skin's needs.

What is the main difference between hair wax and hair cream pomade?

Hair wax is usually thicker and provides a very stiff hold with a greasy finish, often difficult to wash out. Hair cream pomade has a softer texture, providing a flexible, invisible hold that is water-soluble and easier on the hair strands.

Will hair cream pomade cause acne on my forehead?

Our hair cream pomade is designed to be non-greasy and uses beeswax rather than heavy petroleum. Because it is water-soluble and easy to wash off, it significantly reduces the risk of forehead breakouts compared to traditional heavy waxes.

Can I use KWAN YEE GOR pomade on wet hair?

Yes, applying the product to damp hair will result in a slightly higher sheen and a more "slick" look. For a completely matte, natural finish, apply a small amount to dry hair and work it through with your fingers.

How often should I wash out my styling cream?

Because the formula is water-soluble, it should be washed out daily. Leaving any product in your hair overnight can lead to dust accumulation, though our formula is much safer for overnight wear than traditional petroleum waxes.

While camellia butter is not a direct growth stimulant, it is rich in antioxidants and fatty acids that strengthen the hair shaft. Stronger hair is less prone to breakage, which helps you maintain thickness and length over time.

Does this product work for curly hair?

Absolutely. The shea butter in the formula helps to define curls and tame frizz without making them feel crunchy. It provides the hydration curly hair needs while maintaining the shape of the curl.
