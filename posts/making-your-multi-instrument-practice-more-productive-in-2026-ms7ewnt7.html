Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to hang a guitar on the wall?

The best way to hang a guitar is using a wall mount that supports the instrument by the headstock, allowing it to hang vertically. This utilizes gravity to keep the instrument stable. For multi-guitar setups, using a rail-based wall mount guitar bracket is superior as it allows you to adjust the spacing between instruments and minimize the number of holes drilled into your wall.

Is it better to hang a guitar or use a stand?

Hanging a guitar is generally better for both safety and space management. Floor stands are prone to being tipped over and take up valuable floor real estate. A wall mount guitar bracket keeps the instrument out of the "danger zone" where pets, children, or vacuum cleaners might cause damage, while also keeping your floor clear for other gear like keyboards.

What is the best guitar wall mount?

The best guitar wall mount is one that combines structural integrity with finish protection. Look for a heavy duty wall mount guitar bracket made of steel with silicone-coated hooks. Modular systems, like GearHooks, are often considered the best because they grow with your collection and offer unparalleled space efficiency, holding up to 9 guitars in just one meter.

Is a guitar wall hanger safe?

It's generally accepted as a safe way to hang a guitar because the downward exertion from the weight of the guitar isn't nearly as strong as the pull of the strings in the opposite direction. Furthermore, a high-quality wall mount guitar bracket ensures the neck is cradled securely, preventing any warping or structural stress on the instrument.
