Frequently Asked Questions

Is pomade basically clay?

While both are styling products, pomade and clay serve different purposes. Pomades, especially a high shine pomade, offer a slicker, more polished look with light reflection, whereas clays typically provide a matte finish and more gritty texture. In 2026, professionals prefer pomades for formal or sleek styles and clays for messy, casual looks.

What type of pomade feels the most slick and greasy?

Traditional oil-based pomades are known for their slick, heavy, and sometimes greasy feel because they don't dry down. However, a modern water-based high shine pomade provides that same polished aesthetic without the greasy residue or difficult wash-out process. It gives you the shine of a grease with the cleanliness of a gel.

How long does hair pomade last?

A premium strong hold hair pomade is designed to last the entire day, typically from 10 to 14 hours. Higher quality formulas like those with plant-based nourishment maintain their hold without flaking, even in humid conditions. In 2026, most men find that a single application in the morning is sufficient for all-day style.

Can pomade be used on wet hair?

Yes, pomade can be used on wet hair, and doing so is actually a secret trick for maximum shine. Applying high shine pomade to damp hair helps lock in the moisture and allows for a more even distribution of the product. Just be aware that applying to wet hair may slightly reduce the overall hold strength compared to dry hair application.
