Frequently Asked Questions

What does a shave bar do?

A shave bar serves multiple purposes: it cleanses the skin of oils and dirt while providing a lubricating layer that allows a razor to glide smoothly. By softening the hair at the root, it helps achieve a closer shave with significantly less irritation than traditional foams. It is specifically designed to replace both your face wash and your shaving cream in a single, compact tool.

How to make a shave bar at home?

While you can make a shave bar at home using a base of glycerin soap, bentonite clay, and essential oils, it is difficult to achieve the professional balance of ingredients like kaolin clay and citric acid found in high-end products. Commercial bars are dermatologically tested to ensure they provide the exact pH balance required for facial skin. For most users, purchasing a pre-formulated stick is safer and more effective for preventing razor burn.

How long does a shave bar last?

A standard 40g shave bar is highly concentrated and can last significantly longer than a traditional aerosol can of shaving cream. Depending on the frequency of use, one stick can typically last between 2 to 4 months. Because you only apply exactly what you need directly to the skin, there is zero product waste compared to foams that often end up down the drain.

Is it better to trim pubic hair before or after a shower?

It is generally better to trim or shave hair after or during a warm shower. The warm water and steam soften the hair follicles and open the pores, making the hair much easier to cut. Using a shave bar during this process adds an extra layer of protection, ensuring the skin remains hydrated and reducing the risk of painful ingrown hairs or razor bumps in sensitive areas.
