Fast Installation: Setting Up Your Modular Wall Rack in Under 10 Minutes

The thought of measuring, leveling, and drilling individual wall hangers for five different guitars is enough to trigger procrastination. High-performance rails eliminate that tedious process. With only three anchor holes required per 1-metre section, you achieve robust security across masonry, timber studs, or concrete in a fraction of the time.

Step 1: Determine Wall Height and Stud Alignment

Position your rail approximately 1.5 to 1.8 metres off the floor, allowing ample drop clearance for extended-scale basses and strap buttons. Use a simple spirit level to align the mounting track. If mounting into drywall over wooden battens, line up the heavy-duty hex head bolts directly with wall studs for maximum shear capacity.

Step 2: Secure the 3-Hole Rail Foundation

Drill three holes corresponding to the rail’s fixing slots. Insert the expanding nylon anchors into brick, stone, or block walls. Drive the heavy-duty hex bolts home using an Allen key. The galvanized rail locks flush against the wall, creating an unyielding spine ready to support gear immediately.

Step 3: Slide and Lock Custom Instrument Hooks

Choose the best guitar hooks tailored to your specific gear rotation. Slide the hook attachments directly into the rail’s slots and tighten them into place. As your subscription delivery changes each month, simply loosen the hook clamp and slide it sideways to create wider or narrower gaps—no power tools needed.
