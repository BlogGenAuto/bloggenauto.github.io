FAQ

What is the difference between pomade and gel?

Pomade offers a more natural, flexible hold, often with nourishing ingredients, while gel provides a firmer, often crunchy hold that can include harsher chemicals.

How do water-based pomades benefit hair health?

Water-based pomades are easier to wash out, reducing residue and build-up on the scalp, and often include nourishing ingredients that support hair health.

Are plant-based pomades effective for all hair types?

Yes, plant-based pomades are generally suitable for all hair types due to their gentle, nourishing formulas.

Can pomades achieve the same hold as gels?

Yes, modern strong hold pomades can achieve the same, if not better, hold as gels without the stiffness or flaking.
