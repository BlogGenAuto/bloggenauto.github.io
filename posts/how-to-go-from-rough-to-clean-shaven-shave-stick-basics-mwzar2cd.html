Step-by-Step Shaving Stick for Men Morning Routine

Mastering a quick, effective clean shave takes less than five minutes when using the right technique. Implementing a streamlined shaving stick for men cuts out redundant steps while upgrading skin comfort.

  1. Hydrate the Face: Splash warm water generously across your jawline, neck, and cheeks for 30 seconds to open pores and soften bristles.
  2. Twist and Glide: Twist up the product a few millimeters and massage the solid formula across damp skin in circular motions.
  3. Lather Up: Use wet fingertips or a damp brush to transform the clear layer into a rich, lubricating lather.
  4. Perform the Shave: Shave with light, short strokes in the direction of hair growth. For stubborn coarse spots, reapply a quick swipe of the stick.
  5. Rinse and Revitalize: Rinse thoroughly with cold water to close pores and trigger the soothing menthol cooling sensation.
