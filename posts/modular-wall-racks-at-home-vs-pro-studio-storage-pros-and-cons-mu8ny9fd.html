Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hang my guitar on the wall without drilling?

Technically, yes, you can use high-strength adhesive hangers or tension-based systems for very light instruments. However, these are generally not recommended for expensive or heavy guitars as the bond can fail over time. For maximum security, a guitar wall mount without screws is rarely as safe as a rail system that uses minimal, discreet fixings.

Is there a guitar wall mount that doesn't require a drill?

Some products use specialized adhesive strips or over-the-door hooks that do not require a drill. While convenient for renters, these often have low weight limits and lack the modularity of professional systems. If you are looking for a compromise, consider a freestanding rack that mimics the aesthetics of a wall rail without the need for drilling.

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are highly recommended for guitars as they keep instruments off the floor and away from pets or foot traffic. They also allow for better air circulation around the instrument, which is vital for maintaining proper humidity levels in 2026. Professional wall mounts also double as a display, turning your collection into a functional piece of art.

Can command strips hold guitars?

While Command strips are excellent for pictures and light décor, they are typically not rated for the weight and constant vibration of a guitar. Most electric guitars weigh between 3-5kg, and acoustic guitars can be bulky. Using an unrated adhesive is a significant risk to your instrument's safety; professional rails are a much more reliable investment.
