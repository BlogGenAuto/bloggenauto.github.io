Frequently Asked Questions

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are excellent for guitars as they keep them off the floor and away from pets, children, and vacuum cleaners. High-quality mounts with silicone padding are completely safe for the instrument's finish and neck structure.

Is it better to hang a guitar or keep it in a case?

While cases offer protection for travel, hanging a guitar on the wall is often better for regular use as it encourages more frequent playing. Furthermore, wall mounting allows for better air circulation around the instrument, which is beneficial for maintaining consistent wood humidity in a controlled studio environment.

What is the best way to hang a guitar on the wall?

The best way to hang a guitar is by using a weight-rated rail system that is securely anchored into wall studs. A rail-based system is superior to individual hooks because it distributes the weight across a larger area and requires fewer drill holes, maintaining the structural integrity of your wall.

How safe are guitar wall mounts?

Modern guitar wall mounts are incredibly safe, provided they are engineered from durable materials like steel and installed correctly. Many professional systems now include security straps and non-slip coatings to prevent the instrument from shifting or falling, even in high-traffic areas.
