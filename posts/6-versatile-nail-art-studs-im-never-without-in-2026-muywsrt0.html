Solving Common 3D Nail Problems

1. Snagging: Use a thick top coat to "encapsulate" the edges of the metal starfish studs.
2. Falling Off: Use a dedicated rhinestone glue or a bead of builder gel.
3. Weight: Place larger studs closer to the center or base of the nail to maintain balance.
4. Fading: Avoid harsh cleaning chemicals without wearing gloves.
5. Removal: Use a specialized nipper to pop the stud off before soaking in acetone.
