    The key is to use light, targeted strokes. Instead of combing from the root every time, focus on the areas that have shifted or frizzed. The oil-infused material will help glide through existing pomade without clumping it.

    Is an anti-static comb really better than wood or metal?

    Yes, especially for wedding environments. While wood is good, it can absorb moisture and warp. Metal can be harsh on the scalp. The KWAN YEE GOR anti-static resin provides the perfect balance of flexibility, durability, and frizz control.

    Does the oil-infused material make my hair greasy?

    No. The oil is embedded within the material to ensure a smooth glide and reduce friction; it does not transfer a greasy residue to your hair. It simply helps distribute the hair's own natural oils more effectively.

    What is the 30-30-40 rule exactly?

    It is a strategy for event grooming: 30% of your look is the morning preparation, 30% is the quality of your portable tools, and 40% is the discipline of performing 30-second touch-ups at key intervals (like before photos or dinner).
