How to Install Guitar Wall Storage Safely and Securely

Installation is the most daunting part for many. However, a modern wall mount guitar hangers setup using a rail system simplifies the process by requiring fewer holes in the wall while providing more support.

Finding Your Foundation

Always aim for wall studs when mounting heavy rails. If studs aren't available, use high-quality heavy-duty wall anchors. The goal is to distribute the weight of multiple guitars—which can easily exceed 20kg—across a wide area of the wall surface.

Fine-Tuning the Gap

Standard electric guitars usually require a 45mm gap at the nut. For 12-string or Spanish guitars, choose wall mount guitar hangers that allow for prong adjustment. Gently spreading the prongs ensures the wider neck sits securely without scratching the headstock.

Yes, wall hangers are an excellent and safe way to store guitars as long as they use high-quality materials and are mounted correctly.
