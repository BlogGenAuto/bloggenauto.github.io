FAQ

How do I remove 3D nail decorations?

To remove 3D nail decorations, soak your nails in acetone-based remover for a few minutes. Use a cuticle stick to gently lift the decorations off your nails.

Are metal nail studs safe for natural nails?

Yes, metal nail studs are safe when used with a protective base coat. Always follow application instructions to minimize damage to your natural nails.

What makes ocean theme nails unique?

Ocean theme nails incorporate elements from the sea, such as shells and starfish, offering a unique, nature-inspired look that is both trendy and timeless.

Can I apply these decorations on gel nails?

Yes, 3D decorations can be applied to both gel and traditional nail polish. Ensure the polish is completely dry before application.

How do I maintain 3D nail art?

Maintain your 3D nail art by applying a top coat every few days to keep the decorations secure and looking fresh.
