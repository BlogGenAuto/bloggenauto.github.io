Yes! Because this is a lightweight, non-greasy hair wax, it won't weigh down fine or thinning hair. Applying it as a pre-styler can actually help create the illusion of more volume and thickness.

No, the scent profile of KWAN YEE GOR pomade is designed to be a subtle, calming woody fragrance. It provides a clean aroma that is noticeable only to those close to you, making it perfect for professional 2026 settings.

Absolutely not. One of the main benefits of a water-soluble pomade is that it breaks down easily with water. Regular rinsing or a standard gentle shampoo is more than enough to leave your hair clean and product-free.
