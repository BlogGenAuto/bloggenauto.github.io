Frequently Asked Questions

Is shaving with a safety razor better?

Yes, shaving with a safety razor is generally better for your skin and the environment. Because it uses a single, extremely sharp blade, it cuts hair at the surface without tugging the skin, which significantly reduces razor burn and ingrown hairs. Additionally, it is a zero-waste alternative to plastic-heavy disposable systems.

What are the disadvantages of a safety razor?

The main disadvantage is the initial learning curve, as users must learn to maintain a proper angle and use light pressure. However, most men master this within three to five shaves. Additionally, while the razor itself is an investment, the ongoing cost of blades is much lower than cartridge alternatives.

Why did safety razors lose popularity?

Safety razors lost popularity in the mid-20th century due to the aggressive marketing of disposable, multi-blade cartridges that prioritized convenience and speed over shave quality. However, in 2026, they have seen a massive resurgence as men rediscover the benefits of traditional wet shaving for better skin health and environmental sustainability.
