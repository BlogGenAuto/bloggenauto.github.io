These products are designed to reflect light, giving your hair a polished, semi-gloss finish that looks healthy and sharp throughout the day. The level of shine can often be adjusted by applying the product to wetter or drier hair.

What's the best pomade for men's hair?

The best pomade for men's hair in 2026 is one that balances a strong, all-day hold with nourishing, plant-based ingredients. Look for water-soluble options that provide a clean finish without greasiness, such as the KWAN YEE GOR Strong Hold Pomade. What's the difference between pomade and wax?

Pomade typically provides more shine and a slicker finish, making it ideal for classic styles like pompadours or side parts. Wax usually offers a more matte finish and a "tackier" texture for messy, textured looks. While waxes are great for volume, a high shine pomade is the superior choice for a professional, polished aesthetic.

Is pomade better for your hair?

Pomade can be better for your hair if it contains natural extracts and is water-based, as it avoids the harsh chemicals and difficult removal of older oil-based formulas. Modern high-quality pomades actually work to fortify the hair shaft and protect the scalp while providing the necessary hold for your style.
