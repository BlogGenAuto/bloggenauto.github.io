Frequently Asked Questions

Can I hang my guitar on drywall?

Yes, you can hang a guitar on drywall, but it requires the right hardware. Using heavy-duty hex bolts into wooden battens behind the plasterboard is the safest method. A modular rail system is ideal for drywall because it distributes the weight of multiple instruments across several studs rather than putting all the stress on one small point.

How can I make a DIY guitar holder for my wall?

While you can create a holder using wood blocks or rope, these often lack the durability needed for long-term storage. For a professional DIY approach, it is better to purchase a pre-engineered steel rail and then customize the hook layout yourself. This gives you the "built-it-yourself" satisfaction with industrial-grade safety and a 100kg weight rating.

Is it better to hang or stand a guitar?

Hanging a guitar is generally superior for space management and safety in busy environments. Floor stands are easily knocked over and take up valuable square footage. Hanging your instrument on the wall keeps it away from pets, vacuums, and foot traffic while keeping it perfectly accessible for practice.

How high should I hang my guitar on the wall?

The ideal height is usually at chest level (approximately 1.5 to 1.7 metres from the floor) for easy access. However, if you are using the rail for display purposes in a high-ceiling room, you can mount it higher. Always ensure you can reach the instrument comfortably without needing a ladder, unless it is a permanent display piece.
