Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to hang a guitar on the wall?

The best way to hang a guitar on the wall is using a secure, wall-mounted rail system that anchors into studs or masonry. This method provides the most stability and allows for easy adjustment of instruments as your collection grows. Using a rail also minimizes the number of holes you need to drill into your wall compared to individual hangers.

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are excellent for guitars as they keep instruments in a stable environment away from floor-level hazards. Hanging a guitar by its headstock is a safe and industry-standard method used by professionals and music shops globally. It encourages more frequent playing by keeping your instruments visible and easily accessible.

How do I hang my guitar on the wall?

To hang your guitar on the wall, first identify the type of wall you have (drywall, brick, or wood) and choose the appropriate fixings. Mark your holes using a level, drill the necessary pilot holes, and secure a heavy-duty rail or mount. Once the mount is firm, simply slide your guitar's headstock into the protective hook and ensure it is centered.

How to mount a guitar on a wall without drilling?

While there are adhesive-based options, they are generally not recommended for the weight and value of musical instruments. If you want to minimize drilling, use a single modular rail system like GearHooks® which only requires 3 holes to support up to 100kg of gear. This is much safer than non-drill methods which often fail due to temperature or humidity changes affecting the adhesive.
