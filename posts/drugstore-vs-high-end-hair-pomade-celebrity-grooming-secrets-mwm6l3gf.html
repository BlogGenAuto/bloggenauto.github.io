How to Apply Water Soluble Pomade for Men for All-Day Architectural Hold

The best product will underperform if applied incorrectly. Achieving an all-day architectural shape requires proper emulsification and strategic placement from roots to ends.

  1. Scoop Sparingly: Start with an almond-sized amount of hair pomade. A high-density formula delivers immense holding strength with very little mass.
  2. Emulsify Completely: Rub the pomade vigorously between your palms and fingers until it turns completely clear. The friction warms the plant resins for seamless transfer.
  3. Work Root to Tip: Apply the formula starting at the back of the head, moving forward. Work through the roots first to create structural lift, then finish the surface lengths.
  4. Comb and Finish: Use a high-quality styling comb to distribute the product evenly and set the parted lines. Enjoy a sharp, clean rinse finish that washes out effortlessly at night.
