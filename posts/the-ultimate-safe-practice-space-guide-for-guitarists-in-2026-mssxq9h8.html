Frequently Asked Questions about Guitar Storage

Is it okay to hang an acoustic guitar on the wall?

Yes, hanging an acoustic guitar on a high-quality acoustic guitar wall mount is perfectly safe and often recommended by professionals. The weight of the guitar is supported by the strongest part of the neck, the headstock joint, which is designed to withstand far more tension from the strings themselves. Just ensure the mount uses non-reactive materials like silicone to protect the finish.

Is it better for guitars to hang or stand?

Hanging is generally considered superior for both safety and space-saving. Floor stands are easily knocked over and expose the guitar to floor-level hazards like dust, pets, and temperature fluctuations near the ground. A wall mount keeps the instrument in a stable, elevated position where it is less likely to suffer accidental impacts.

How safe are guitar wall mounts?

Modern acoustic guitar wall mount systems are incredibly safe when installed correctly into studs or solid masonry. Rail-based systems like GearHooks are even safer because they distribute weight across a larger area. With the addition of security straps on longer hooks, your instruments remain firmly in place even in high-traffic areas.

How can I display my guitar on the wall?

You can display your guitar vertically using a standard hook, or use a guitar wall mount sideways configuration to fit more instruments in a tighter space. Using a rail system allows you to create creative layouts like 'waves' or 'zig-zags,' turning your instrument collection into a piece of functional wall art in your home or studio.
