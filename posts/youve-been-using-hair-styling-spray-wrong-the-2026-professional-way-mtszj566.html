Is a precision mist better than an aerosol for fine hair?

Yes, precision mist is significantly better for fine hair. Aerosols can be too heavy and concentrated, which often weighs down fine strands. A precision hair spray allows you to apply a very light, even layer that provides structure without the weight, helping you maintain volume all day.
