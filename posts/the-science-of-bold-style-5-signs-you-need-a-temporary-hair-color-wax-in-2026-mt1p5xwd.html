Temporary hair color wax is designed to last until your next shampoo. It is highly resistant to sweat and daily movement but will rinse away easily with warm water and soap, making it the perfect zero-commitment styling tool.

No, quite the opposite. High-quality products like Kwan Yee Gor include keratin hair care and squalene for hair, which actually moisturize and strengthen your strands while providing vibrant color.

Can I use this on dark or black hair?

Yes! Our colored hair wax is designed with high-pigment density, meaning the creamy texture provides full coverage even on naturally dark hair without the need for pre-bleaching or lighting.

Is the formula truly fragrance-free?

Yes, we prioritize men with sensitive skin. Our fragrance-free hair color avoids synthetic perfumes that often lead to scalp irritation, making it suitable for all skin types and those who prefer no strong scents.

Does the color transfer onto clothes or pillows?

Once the wax has dried completely on your hair (usually within a few minutes), it is smudge-resistant. However, because it is a water-soluble hair dye, we recommend avoiding heavy rain or sleeping with it on to prevent any potential transfer to light-colored fabrics.

How do I apply the wax for the best results?

Start with clean, dry hair. Take a small amount of wax, rub it between your palms to soften the texture, and then work it through your hair from roots to tips. You can add more layers for a more vibrant instant hair transformation.
