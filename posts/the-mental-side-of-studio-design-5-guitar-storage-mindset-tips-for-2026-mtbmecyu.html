Frequently Asked Questions

Are wall mounts okay for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are perfectly safe and often superior to floor stands. When properly installed into studs or using professional rail systems like GearHooks, they provide a stable environment that keeps instruments away from foot traffic, pets, and accidental tips. High-quality mounts with silicone padding also ensure no chemical damage occurs to the guitar's neck.

What is the best way to hang guitars on the wall?

The best way to hang guitars is by using a modular rail system. This method distributes the weight across a horizontal rail, allowing for lateral adjustments as your collection grows. For 2026, the gold standard includes using 45mm gap double-prong hooks with silicone sleeves to ensure a secure, non-slip grip that accommodates both electric and acoustic widths.

How can I display my guitar on the wall?

You can display your guitar vertically, at an angle, or even sideways depending on the hook type. For a gallery-style guitar wall decor look, use staggered heights to overlap the bodies slightly, which saves horizontal space while maintaining a beautiful visual profile. Adding LED backlighting or accent spots can further enhance the professional "tech shop" aesthetic.

Is it better to hang a guitar or keep it in a case?

While cases offer the ultimate protection from humidity swings, they create a "barrier to entry" for practice. Hanging a guitar is better for most active musicians because it encourages daily use. If you live in a particularly dry or humid climate, maintaining room-wide humidity is a small price to pay for the creative benefits of having your instruments immediately accessible on the wall.
