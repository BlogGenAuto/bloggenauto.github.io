Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to hang or stand a guitar?

Hanging a guitar on a professional guitar stand wall mount is generally superior for both space-saving and neck health. Unlike floor stands, which can put pressure on the lower bout or be easily knocked over, a wall mount keeps the instrument safe from pets, vacuums, and accidental kicks. In a 2026 studio environment, vertical hanging on a steel wall rail is the preferred method for most professionals.

Are wall mounts okay for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are highly recommended, provided they are high-quality and properly installed. Using a heavy duty storage rail like GearHooks ensures that the weight is distributed across multiple points, preventing the risk of the mount pulling out of the wall. Professional tech shops have used wall-mounted systems for decades to keep high-value instruments safe during storage.

How to mount a guitar stand on a wall?

Mounting a guitar stand wall mount starts with locating the studs in a timber wall or using the correct masonry fixings for brick. With a GearRail, you only need to drill three holes to secure a 1-meter rail. Once the rail is bolted using the provided hex head bolts, you can simply clip on your hooks. This modular approach is much simpler and more secure than trying to align multiple individual hooks.

Is it okay to leave a guitar on a stand?

Leaving a guitar on a high-quality guitar stand wall mount is perfectly safe for long-term storage. However, you should ensure the environment is humidity-controlled, especially for acoustic instruments. Using a wall rail keeps the guitar away from the dampness often found near floor level, providing a more stable environment for the wood and finish.
