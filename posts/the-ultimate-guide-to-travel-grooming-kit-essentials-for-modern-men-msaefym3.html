What is the difference between pomade and wax for travel?

Wax often provides a matte look but is notoriously difficult to wash out in hotel sinks. Pomade, specifically water-based versions, offers a similar or stronger hold with a natural shine and the ultimate travel benefit: easy removal. This makes pomade the superior choice for a travel grooming kit.
