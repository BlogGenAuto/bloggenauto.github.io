Frequently Asked Questions

Is a wall mount guitar stand safe for heavy electric basses?

Yes, especially when using a steel rail system like GearHooks®. While individual plastic hangers might fail, a steel rail spreads the load across multiple wall fixings, comfortably supporting instruments up to 20kg per hook and 100kg per rail.

Can I install the GearRail on a plasterboard wall?

The GearRail is ideally fixed to brick, block, or wooden studs. If mounting on plasterboard, we recommend fixing the rail into the vertical timber battens (studs) using the provided hex head bolts for maximum security.

Will the hooks damage my guitar's finish?

Our hooks are designed with protective coatings to prevent scratches. However, for vintage nitrocellulose finishes, we always recommend checking the compatibility of any storage solution, as these finishes are famously sensitive to long-term contact.

How many guitars can I fit on a 1-meter rail?

Typically, you can fit 3 to 5 guitars depending on whether you mount them flat against the wall or at an angle. The modular nature allows you to slide the hooks to find the perfect density for your specific space.
