Frequently Asked Questions

Is it better to hang or stand a guitar?

Hanging a guitar is generally better for both space management and safety. It keeps the instrument away from floor-level hazards like vacuum cleaners, pets, and accidental kicks. Furthermore, a high-quality guitar stands wall mount allows the neck to hang naturally, preventing the side-loading pressure that some leaning floor stands can exert on the neck joint.

Are wall mounts okay for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are perfectly safe for guitars provided they are installed correctly into appropriate wall materials. Professional systems are designed to support the weight of even the heaviest 1970s Les Pauls. For 2026, ensure your mount uses non-reactive materials like silicone to avoid damaging delicate finishes.

How to mount a guitar stand on a wall?

To mount a professional rail-based system, you typically only need to drill three 8mm holes. After inserting the wall plugs, you screw the rail into place. The guitar stands wall mount hooks then slide onto the rail and are tightened into your desired position. This is much faster than individual mounts which require 3-4 holes per guitar.

Is it bad to lean a guitar against a wall?

Leaning a guitar against a wall is one of the primary causes of headstock breaks and neck warping. Gravity constantly pulls at the instrument, often causing it to slide sideways or putting uneven pressure on the tuning pegs. Utilizing acoustic guitar hooks or an electric guitar display rack eliminates this risk entirely by providing a secure, vertical storage point.
