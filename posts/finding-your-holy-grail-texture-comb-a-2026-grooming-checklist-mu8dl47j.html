FAQ

What is a texture comb?

A texture comb is a specialized tool designed to add volume and definition to hair. It helps in lifting the roots and creating separation among hair strands for a textured look.

Can I use a texture comb on wet hair?

Yes, many texture combs, including the KWAN YEE GOR Texture Comb, are designed for use on both wet and dry hair, making them versatile tools in your grooming kit.

How does a texture comb prevent frizz?

Texture combs made with anti-static materials minimize static electricity, which is a common cause of frizz, allowing for smoother styling.

Are there any specific hair types that benefit more from texture combs?

Texture combs are ideal for all hair types, but they are particularly beneficial for individuals with fine or thin hair looking to add volume and fullness.
