The 30-30-40 Seasonal Organization Routine

The 30% Primary Rotation

Dedicate 30% of your rail space to your 'Daily Drivers.' These are the instruments you reach for every day. Place these in the centre of your rack at the most ergonomic height for quick access during practice or recording sessions.

The 30% Secondary Layer

The next 30% is for secondary gear—instruments used for specific tones or backup. These can be placed slightly higher or at the ends of the rail. Because GearHooks® allows you to slide hooks along the rail, you can adjust the spacing to accommodate different body widths without effort.

The 40% Expansion and Accessories

The final 40% of your guitar wall mount rack capacity should be reserved for future acquisitions or accessory storage. This space is perfect for hanging heavy-duty cables, straps, or even protective gig bags, keeping your floor completely clear of clutter.
