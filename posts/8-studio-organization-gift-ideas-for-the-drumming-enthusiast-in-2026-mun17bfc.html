Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay to hang guitars on the wall?

Yes, it is perfectly safe to hang guitars on the wall as long as you use a high-quality mount that supports the neck properly. In fact, keeping them on the wall protects them from accidental bumps on the floor and encourages more frequent practice. Using a modular rail system adds an extra layer of security by anchoring multiple instruments to a heavy-duty steel beam.

What is the best way to hang guitars on the wall?

The best way to hang guitars is by using a modular rail system like the GearRail. This method allows you to adjust the spacing between instruments without drilling new holes every time you buy a new guitar. It also ensures the weight is distributed across multiple wall fixings, making it much more stable than single-screw hangers.

How do I hang my guitar on the wall?

To hang your guitar safely, first identify your wall type (brick, drywall, or timber). Use the appropriate fixings—like the expanding nylon fixings provided with GearHooks—and ensure the mount is level. If you're using a rail, you simply secure the rail with three bolts and then slide your guitar hooks for wall into the desired position.
