Frequently Asked Questions

How to hang a guitar sideways on the wall?

To hang a guitar sideways, you need a specialized guitar side wall mount that supports the instrument by the neck or body at an angle. Modern modular systems allow you to rotate the hook and stagger the depth (using 200mm or 300mm hooks) so the guitars overlap slightly, significantly increasing storage density without the instruments touching.

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are perfectly safe for guitars as long as they are installed correctly into wall studs or solid masonry. Professional mounts use silicone padding to protect the finish and are engineered to support the weight of the instrument at the strongest point—the headstock and neck joint. In fact, keeping them on the wall prevents them from being knocked over as they might be on a floor stand.

Is it better to hang a guitar or keep it in a case?

While cases offer the best protection against extreme humidity swings, hanging a guitar on a guitar side wall mount is superior for musical progress and creative flow. In 2026, the consensus among professionals is that an accessible guitar is a played guitar. If your studio is climate-controlled, a wall mount provides the best balance of safety and accessibility.

Is leaning a guitar against a wall bad for it?

Yes, leaning a guitar against a wall is dangerous. It puts unnecessary pressure on the neck and headstock, which can lead to warping over time, and it is the most common cause of broken headstocks due to the guitar sliding off the wall. A dedicated guitar wall mount sideways or front-facing hook provides a secure, mechanical lock that eliminates the risk of gravity-related accidents.
