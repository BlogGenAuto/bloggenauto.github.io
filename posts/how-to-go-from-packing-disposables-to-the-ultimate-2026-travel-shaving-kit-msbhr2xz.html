Frequently Asked Questions

Is an adjustable safety razor better than a cartridge razor for travel?

Yes, an adjustable safety razor is superior because it allows you to customize the blade gap based on your current skin condition and beard growth, which is essential when traveling through different climates. Furthermore, it is more sustainable and cost-effective over time.

Can I take safety razor blades on a plane in 2026?

According to current airline regulations, the razor handle itself is allowed in carry-on luggage, but the individual double-edge blades must be packed in your checked baggage. If traveling carry-on only, you should purchase blades at your destination.

What is the best adjustable safety razor for beginners?

The KWAN YEE GOR adjustable razor is highly recommended for beginners because of its 7-phase adjustment system. Starting at level 1 provides a very mild and safe shave, allowing users to build confidence without the risk of significant nicks.

How do I clean my brass safety razor while traveling?

Simply rinse the razor thoroughly with warm water after each use and leave the butterfly head open to air dry. The brass construction is naturally bacteria-resistant, making it ideal for maintaining hygiene on the go.

How often should I change the Solingen blades in my travel kit?

For the best results and to prevent irritation, most men find that changing the blade every 5 to 7 shaves is ideal. Since Solingen blades are exceptionally sharp and durable, they tend to maintain their edge longer than standard blades.

Why does an adjustable razor help with razor bumps?

Razor bumps are often caused by blades cutting hair below the skin line or tugging at the follicle. By adjusting the razor to a milder setting, you ensure a clean cut at the surface level, which significantly reduces the risk of ingrown hairs.
