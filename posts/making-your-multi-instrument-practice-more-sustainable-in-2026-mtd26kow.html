Are slatwall guitar hooks safe for nitrocellulose finishes?

Yes, quality hooks like GearHooks use silicone sleeves. Unlike rubber or certain plastics, silicone does not react with the nitrocellulose lacquer used on vintage and high-end guitars, ensuring your finish remains intact over years of storage.

How much weight can a wall-mounted guitar rack hold?

A professionally designed rack like the GTR series is engineered to hold up to 9 guitars. By distributing the weight across a steel rail mounted with heavy-duty fixings, it easily supports the combined weight of electrics and acoustics, provided it is installed according to instructions.

Can I store different instruments like ukuleles on the same rack?

Absolutely. The modular nature of slatwall guitar hooks means you can mix and match. You can have a heavy bass, an acoustic guitar, and several ukuleles or mandolins on the same rail by simply adjusting the spacing of the hooks.

Is it difficult to install a guitar wall rail?

No. Most modular systems require far less work than individual hangers. For example, a rack for 9 guitars requires only 3 holes to be drilled, whereas 9 separate hooks would require 36 holes, significantly reducing the margin for error and wall damage.
