Frequently Asked Questions

How to hang a guitar on the wall sideways?

To hang a guitar sideways, you need a specialized horizontal mount that supports both the neck and the body. By using a modular rail system, you can position two hooks—one for the neck and one for the lower bout—along the rail to match the specific length of your guitar perfectly. This ensures the instrument sits level and remains securely balanced against the wall.

Horizontal Guitar Wall Mount. Our Horizontal Guitar Wall Mount is the sleek and efficient way to display your guitars on their side. Do guitar wall hangers damage guitars?

High-quality hangers made from guitar-safe materials (like those used in GearHooks) do not damage instruments. However, cheap rubber or plastic hangers can react with nitrocellulose finishes found on vintage guitars. Always ensure your guitar wall mount sideways system uses non-reactive padding and provides sturdy support that doesn't put undue pressure on the tuning pegs.

Is it bad to lean your guitar against the wall?

Yes, leaning a guitar against a wall is risky and can lead to structural damage. It puts unnecessary pressure on the neck and is the leading cause of "guitar tip-overs," which often result in snapped headstocks. A guitar wall mount sideways or vertical mount provides a stable, permanent home that keeps the instrument away from foot traffic and accidental bumps.
