Group Sourcing Circles: Splitting Bulk Lots and Shipping

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to hang guitars on the wall?

The safest and most reliable way to hang guitars on a wall is using a heavy-duty steel rail system anchored securely into wall studs or solid masonry. Unlike standalone hooks that rely on delicate individual fixings, a continuous track distributes instrument weight across multiple points. Choosing modular rails fitted with non-reactive, cushioned hooks also protects your instrument's finish while letting you adjust spacing without drilling additional holes.

What is the best guitar rack stand?

While floor racks work for quick live stage swaps, the best storage rack stand for studios is an engineered, wall-mounted steel rail system. How to build a guitar wall hanger?

To build a secure DIY guitar wall hanger, mount a continuous cold-rolled steel rail across solid wall studs or masonry using heavy-duty hex lag bolts. Once the base rail is level and anchored, attach adjustable steel hanger hooks equipped with protective neck padding. This creates a fully modular system that allows horizontal adjustment without requiring you to drill new holes into your walls.
