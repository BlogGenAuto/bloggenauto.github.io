Frequently Asked Questions

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are highly recommended for guitars as they keep instruments in a stable environment and safe from floor-level accidents. When using a professional-grade guitar wall mount rack, the weight is distributed safely across the wall studs or masonry. Furthermore, keeping them on the wall ensures they stay at a consistent temperature compared to being stored on a cold floor.

How safe are guitar wall mounts?

Guitar wall mounts are incredibly safe provided they are installed correctly into a solid surface and use high-quality materials. Steel mounts with silicone sleeves provide a secure grip without damaging the instrument's neck or finish. For added security, systems like GearHooks include safety straps to prevent the instrument from being bumped off the hook.

Is it better to hang or stand a guitar?

Hanging a guitar is generally considered superior for long-term accessibility and space management. Hanging prevents the "lean" that can occur on some floor stands, which can put unnecessary pressure on the neck over time. Additionally, hanging keeps the guitar away from foot traffic, reducing the risk of it being knocked over.

How can I hang my guitar on the wall?

To hang your guitar safely, start by choosing a heavy-duty rail or individual mount that matches your wall type. Use a level to ensure the rail is straight and secure it using appropriate fixings for brick, concrete, or timber studs. For a high-density setup, use adjustable hooks that allow you to rotate the instrument to the best angle for your space.
