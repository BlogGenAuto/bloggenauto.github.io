Is it good to hang your guitar on the wall?

Yes, hanging your guitar on the wall is an excellent way to save space and keep your instruments safe from floor-level hazards like pets, vacuum cleaners, or accidental kicks. When using a high-quality argos guitar wall mount alternative like GearHooks, you also ensure the neck is supported correctly without undue stress.

What is the best way to hang guitars on the wall?

The best way to hang guitars is using a modular rail system that allows for staggered mounting. This method enables you to overlap the guitars slightly by using hooks of different lengths, fitting more instruments into a smaller area. This high-density approach is far superior to standard individual hooks which require massive amounts of wall space and dozens of drill holes.

Is it better to hang a guitar or keep it in a case?

While cases provide maximum protection against environmental changes, guitars kept in cases are statistically played less often. For daily players, hanging them on a wall rack in a humidity-controlled room is the ideal balance of safety and accessibility. If you have a massive collection, using a guitar wall mount sideways configuration ensures even your "B-rig" instruments remain part of the room's aesthetic while staying off the floor.

How can I hang my guitar on the wall?

To hang your guitar safely, you need to identify your wall type (masonry or stud) and use the appropriate fixings. For a single argos guitar wall mount, you'll need a drill, level, and screwdriver. For a multi-guitar system like GearHooks, you simply mount the rail with three heavy-duty fixings and then slide on the individual hooks. This modular approach allows you to adjust the spacing as your collection grows or changes.
