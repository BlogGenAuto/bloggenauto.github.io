Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to hang a guitar on the wall?

The best way to hang a guitar is by mounting it to a secure stud or using a heavy-duty rail system. Once the mounting rail or plate is securely attached to the wall using expanding nylon fixings or hex head bolts, you can place the instrument in the yoke. This ensures the weight is distributed properly and the instrument won't fall, providing a much more stable solution than simple nails or adhesive hooks.

Is it safe to hang guitars on a wall?

Hanging guitars on the wall is perfectly safe as long as the wall is dry, structurally sound, and the instruments are not in a high-traffic corridor. Storing guitars on walls is actually preferred by many professionals because it keeps them away from floor-level hazards like prying fingers or pets. Using a high-capacity guitar wall rail further increases safety by providing a robust steel anchor that resists pull-out forces better than individual hangers.

Is it better for guitars to hang or stand?

Many musicians find that hanging guitars is superior for long-term storage and display, as it keeps the instrument's neck in a natural position and prevents accidental knocks. Floor stands are convenient for immediate use during a session, but they take up valuable floor space and are prone to tipping. For a 2026 studio, a wall-mounted system offers the best balance of safety, aesthetics, and space efficiency.

Is it okay to lean a guitar on a wall?

It is generally advised to avoid leaning a guitar against a wall. Leaning can lead to accidental tipovers, which often result in broken headstocks or finish scratches. Furthermore, pressure on the tuning keys can cause the instrument to go out of tune or even damage the tuning mechanism. A dedicated easy install wall mount is a much safer and more professional alternative that protects your investment.
