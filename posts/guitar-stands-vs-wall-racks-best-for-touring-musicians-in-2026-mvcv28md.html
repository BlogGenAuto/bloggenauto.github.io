How do you hang guitars on a wall?

To hang guitars safely, you should use a dedicated wall mount that supports the instrument by the headstock or body. In 2026, the most secure method is using a rail-based system that distributes weight across multiple wall studs. Ensure the hooks are padded with silicone or soft material to protect the finish.

What wood is best for guitar?

While hardwoods like mahogany, maple, and rosewood are legendary for guitar construction, they aren't always the best for the mounting bracket itself unless properly reinforced. For a guitar wall mount wood aesthetic, ensure the wood is a dense hardwood like oak or walnut to prevent the screws from stripping over time under the weight of a heavy electric guitar.

How much weight can a guitar wall mount hold?

A standard high-quality guitar wall mount should easily hold 15-20kg, far exceeding the weight of a typical 4-5kg electric guitar.
