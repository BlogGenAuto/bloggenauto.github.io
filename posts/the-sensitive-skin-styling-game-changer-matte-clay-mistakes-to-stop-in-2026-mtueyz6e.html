The Dangerous Application Mistakes Ruining Your Scalp

Even the best matte clay for sensitive skin can cause issues if applied incorrectly. The most common mistake is applying product directly to the scalp rather than the hair strands. This suffocates the hair follicle and can lead to thinning over time.

  • Mistake 1: Applying to dripping wet hair. This dilutes the clay and forces the oils to run onto your skin.
  • Mistake 2: Not emulsifying the product. You must rub the clay between your palms until it disappears. This ensures even distribution and prevents clumps that irritate the scalp.
  • Mistake 3: Skipping the evening rinse. While water-based formulas are safer, leaving any product on overnight can cause friction and irritation.
