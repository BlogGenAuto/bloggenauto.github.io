How does an adjustable safety razor work?

An adjustable safety razor allows you to change the blade gap, which alters the exposure and angle of the blade against your skin. This feature helps tailor the shaving experience to your specific skin type and hair thickness.

Are adjustable razors suitable for beginners?

Yes, adjustable razors are suitable for both beginners and experienced shavers. Beginners can start with a milder setting and gradually adjust to find the ideal sharpness for their skin.

Why choose a brass safety razor?

Brass is durable, resistant to rust and bacteria, making it a long-lasting and hygienic choice. It also aligns with sustainable practices by reducing the need for frequent replacements.
