Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay to hang guitars on a wall?

Yes, hanging guitars on a wall is a highly recommended practice by professionals. It keeps the instrument in a stable, climate-controlled environment away from floor-level hazards like moisture, pets, or accidental bumps. When using a high-quality guitar mount on wall with proper padding, it is the safest way to store your gear for long periods.

Is it better to hang a guitar or use a stand?

In almost all cases, hanging is superior for both safety and space-saving. Floor stands are prone to tipping and consume valuable floor space, whereas a guitar mount on wall utilizes unused vertical space and provides a secure, permanent anchor point. Additionally, hanging displays your instruments as art, which can increase your motivation to practice.

How to mount a guitar on a wall?

To mount a guitar securely, identify a suitable wall area and determine if you need a single hook or a rail system. For a multi-instrument setup, align the modular guitar storage rail, drill the required holes (typically only 3 for a meter-long rail), and secure it with heavy-duty fixings. Finally, slide your hooks into place and adjust the spacing to fit your instruments' body shapes.

Is leaving a guitar on a hanging mount bad?

It's perfectly fine and will not harm the guitar if done properly. It can help keep your guitar safe from damage, easily accessible, out of harm's way like pets and toddlers, and make the best use of space. Just make sure you hang it securely using a dedicated guitar mount on wall that features protective padding like silicone to avoid any chemical reactions with the guitar's finish.
