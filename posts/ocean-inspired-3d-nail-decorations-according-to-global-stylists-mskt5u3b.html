Step-by-Step Application
  1. Apply your base color and cure.
  2. Place a small dot of glue where you want the gold nail charms.
  3. Use a wax picker tool to position the starfish or shell.
  4. Flash cure to set the position, then do a full cure.
  5. Seal the edges with a no-wipe topcoat, ensuring you don't cover the intricate details of the charm itself.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I keep my 3D nail studs from falling off?

The key is using a dedicated charm glue or builder gel rather than standard topcoat. Curing the adhesive under a UV/LED lamp creates a chemical bond that holds heavy metal studs in place for up to 3 weeks.

Are these acrylic nail art supplies reusable?

Yes, if removed carefully with nippers and cleaned with a bit of acetone to remove old glue, these high-quality alloy charms can be used for multiple manicures, making them an eco-friendly choice for 2026.

Will the gold color fade over time?

Premium acrylic nail art supplies use an electroplated finish that is designed to withstand daily wear. However, excessive exposure to harsh cleaning chemicals can wear down the finish, so wearing gloves during housework is recommended.

Can I use these on natural nails?

While possible, 3D charms perform best on gel polish or acrylic extensions. The hard surface of a manicured nail provides a more stable anchor for the metal rivets than the flexible natural nail.

What is the best way to arrange the ocean theme shapes?

Global stylists suggest a 'rule of three'. Grouping a shell, a small rivet, and a starfish in a cluster near the cuticle creates a professional, balanced look that isn't too cluttered.
