Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the right setting on my adjustable safety razor?

Beginners should always start at level 1 or 2. These levels offer minimal blade exposure, making the shave very "mild" and forgiving. As you become more comfortable with the angle and pressure, you can move up to levels 4-7 for a closer shave, especially on thicker hair. Always dial back to a lower setting for sensitive areas like the neck.

How often should I change the German Solingen blades?

For the best results and to avoid irritation, we recommend changing your blade every 4 to 7 shaves. If you feel any "tugging" or if the blade isn't gliding smoothly, it's time for a replacement. Because these blades are very affordable, it is better to change them early than to risk a dull-blade cut.

Can I use this razor if I have sensitive skin?

Absolutely. In fact, an adjustable safety razor is often recommended for sensitive skin. By using a single sharp blade on a low adjustment setting, you reduce the friction and trauma caused by multi-blade razors that often scrape the skin too aggressively.

What is the benefit of a butterfly open design?

The butterfly open (or TTO - Twist To Open) design is all about convenience and safety. Instead of unscrewing the entire head and handling a loose blade, you simply twist the bottom of the handle to open the doors. This makes cleaning and swapping blades much faster and reduces the risk of accidental nicks during maintenance.

Is a full brass razor better than a stainless steel one?

Brass is highly prized in the shaving community for its perfect weight-to-balance ratio and its natural anti-corrosive properties. While stainless steel is also excellent, brass provides a more traditional, heavy feel that helps guide the razor naturally with gravity, which is essential for the no-pressure technique.
