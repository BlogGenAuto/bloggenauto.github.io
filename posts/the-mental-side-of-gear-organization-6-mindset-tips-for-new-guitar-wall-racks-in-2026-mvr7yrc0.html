Is it okay to hang a guitar on the wall?

Yes, it is perfectly safe and often recommended by professionals. Hanging a guitar on the wall by its headstock allows gravity to keep the neck straight and prevents the instrument from being knocked over on the floor. It also keeps the guitar away from pets and children, provided you use a high-quality wall mount for bass guitar or electric guitar with proper wall fixings.
