How often should I use an oil-infused comb?

You can use an oil-infused comb multiple times a day. Unlike applying actual hair oil, which can weigh hair down if overused, the microscopic amounts released by the comb's material provide just enough shine to maintain health without making the hair greasy. It is perfect for those with dry or normal hair types.

Does a pocket comb work for beards?

Absolutely. The fine teeth and anti-static material make it an exceptional tool for beard styling. It helps distribute beard oils evenly from the skin to the tips of the facial hair, reducing itchiness and keeping your beard looking groomed and professional throughout the day.

Why is anti-static material important for men's hair?

Static electricity causes hair strands to repel each other, leading to frizz and an unkempt appearance. Men with short to medium hair often notice this more after wearing hats or in dry office environments. An anti-static comb neutralizes this charge, keeping the hairstyle cohesive and smooth.

How do I clean my professional pocket comb?

To maintain the performance of your oil-infused comb, simply rinse it with lukewarm water and a mild soap once a week. Avoid using harsh chemicals or boiling water, as this could degrade the patented materials. Pat it dry with a soft cloth to maintain the matte black finish.
