Application Tips for Beginners

Start with a clean, dry nail. Use a wax pen or tweezers to pick up the small metal studs. Apply a tiny dot of glue—less is more—to prevent the glue from seeping around the edges of the decoration. This clean application is the hallmark of someone using professional nail art supplies online properly.

The Perfect Seal

To finish the look, use a high-gloss topcoat. While the studs are 3D, a thin layer of topcoat over the edges (not necessarily over the whole metal surface) can help blend the stud into the nail and prevent lifting. This is a common practice recommended by health and safety experts like those at Mayo Clinic for preventing accidental snags.
