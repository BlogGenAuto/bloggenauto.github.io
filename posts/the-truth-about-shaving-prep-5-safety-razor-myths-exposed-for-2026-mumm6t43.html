What does a shave bar do?

A shave bar serves as a highly concentrated lubricant and cleanser designed to soften beard follicles while protecting the skin's surface. Unlike liquid gels, it provides a dense, stable lather that stays on the face longer, improving razor glide. It essentially combines the benefits of a pre-shave oil, a face wash, and a shaving cream into one solid format.

How to make a shave bar at home?

Creating a DIY shave bar involves a process called saponification, where you mix oils like coconut or castor oil with lye. To achieve the necessary "slip" for a safety razor, you would typically add bentonite or kaolin clay and moisturizing agents like shea butter. However, achieving the perfect pH balance for sensitive facial skin is difficult at home, which is why professional dermatologically tested bars are often preferred.

How long does a shave bar last?

Because they do not contain the air and water fillers found in aerosol cans, a 40g shave bar is incredibly efficient. Depending on how often you shave, a single stick can last between 2 to 4 months of daily use. This makes it significantly more cost-effective and environmentally friendly than traditional shaving products.

Is it better to trim pubic hair before or after a shower?

For the best results, you should trim your hair while it is dry before you step into the shower, as dry hair is easier for the clippers to catch. However, if you plan to follow up with a shave using a shave bar, that part of the process should happen after the shower. The warm water and steam help to soften the hair and open the pores for a much smoother finish.
