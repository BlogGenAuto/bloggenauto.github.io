Frequently Asked Questions

What is a water-based pomade?

A water-based pomade is a type of hair styling product that uses water as its primary ingredient, making it easy to apply and wash out. It provides a flexible hold and a natural shine.

How does sodium hyaluronate benefit hair?

Sodium hyaluronate is a powerful humectant that attracts and retains moisture. It helps keep the hair hydrated, reducing dryness and brittleness.

What is a fruity chypre fragrance?

Fruity chypre is a fragrance profile that combines fruity top notes with earthy, woody base notes. It is known for its sophisticated and refreshing aroma.

Can water-based pomades be used on all hair types?

Yes, water-based pomades are versatile and can be used on various hair types, including straight, wavy, and curly hair. They provide a flexible hold without weighing the hair down.

How often should I use pomade?

Pomade can be used daily, but it's essential to wash it out at the end of the day to prevent product buildup and maintain hair health.
