Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay to hang guitars on a wall?

Yes, hanging guitars on a wall is perfectly safe and often recommended by experts at Mayo Clinic for ergonomic workspace design. It keeps the instrument in a stable position, prevents floor-level accidents from pets or children, and ensures the neck remains under consistent tension. Using high-quality guitar wall hooks with proper padding is essential to protect the finish.

What is the best way to hang a guitar on the wall?

The best way to hang a guitar is by using a stud-mounted or heavy-duty rail system that provides security and flexibility. A modular rail like GearHooks® allows you to anchor into the strongest parts of your wall while positioning your instruments exactly where you want them. This professional approach prevents the "swiss cheese" effect of drilling dozens of individual holes as your collection grows.

What can I use to hang guitars on the wall?

You can use individual wall hangers, but for a multi-instrument setup, a modular instrument rack is the superior choice. Look for systems made from cold-rolled steel rather than plastic or thin wood. These systems should include heavy-duty fixings for your specific wall type, whether it's brick, block, or concrete, to ensure the 100kg weight capacity is fully utilized.

Is it better to hang a guitar or use a stand?

Hanging a guitar is generally considered better for both space management and safety. Stands are easily knocked over and take up floor space, while guitar wall hooks keep your instrument in a dedicated "safe zone." Additionally, hanging your guitar makes it more likely you will practice, as the instrument is always accessible and displayed as a piece of art.
