Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What pomade does Leonardo DiCaprio use?

While specific brand endorsements change, Leonardo DiCaprio is famously known for sporting classic, slicked-back styles that require a high-shine, strong hold pomade. Is pomade good for your hair?

High-quality pomades, especially those that are water-based and plant-infused, can actually be beneficial. Formulas containing ingredients like Platycladus and Polygonum Multiflorum Thunb help nourish the scalp and hair follicles. Unlike cheap gels that contain drying alcohols, a premium pomade maintains moisture balance while providing style.

Do I apply pomade to wet or dry hair?

For the best results with a strong hold pomade, apply it to slightly damp, towel-dried hair. Damp hair allows for more even distribution and a smoother finish. If you apply it to bone-dry hair, the hold will be much stronger and stiffer, but it may be harder to comb through without tugging.

Which is better, hair gel or pomade?

Pomade is generally considered superior to hair gel for most men's styles. While gel often leaves hair crunchy, dry, and prone to flaking, pomade offers a flexible yet firm hold with a more natural finish. Furthermore, pomades are re-styleable throughout the day, whereas gel must be washed out once the seal is broken.
