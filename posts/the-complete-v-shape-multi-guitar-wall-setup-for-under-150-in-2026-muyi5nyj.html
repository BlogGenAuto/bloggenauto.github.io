Step-by-Step Technical Installation of Your Guitar Side Wall Mount

One of the biggest deterrents to wall mounting is the fear of drilling dozens of holes. Traditional setups for 9 guitars would require 36 separate holes, increasing the risk of hitting a pipe or wire. A professional guitar side wall mount rail system requires only 3 holes per meter, drastically simplifying the process for DIY enthusiasts.

Preparation and Alignment

First, identify the wall type. Whether it's masonry or stud work, the rail system distributes the weight of up to 9 guitars across the entire length of the steel bar. According to the NIST standards for structural integrity, distributing weight over a larger surface area is significantly safer than point-loading individual hooks. Once you have your 3 points marked, drilling is a 5-minute task.

Adjusting the Sliding Rail

The beauty of the 2026 GearHooks design is the sliding nut technology. Unlike fixed brackets, you can slide your guitar side wall mount hooks along the rail and rotate them to the perfect angle.
