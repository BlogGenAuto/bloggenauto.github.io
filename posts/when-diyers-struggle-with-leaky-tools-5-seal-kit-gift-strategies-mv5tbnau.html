Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know which seal kit size to buy?

The best way to determine the correct size is by checking the equipment’s model number or measuring the existing seal's inner diameter, outer diameter, and thickness with a digital caliper. Most comprehensive O-ring assortment sets cover common metric and imperial sizes used in 2026 home machinery.

Can I reuse old seals if they look okay?

It is never recommended to reuse old rubber gaskets or O-rings. Once a seal has been compressed and exposed to pressure and temperature cycles, it loses its elasticity and won't provide a reliable seal the second time around. Always use a fresh part from a leak repair kit.

What is the difference between rubber and silicone seals?

Rubber seals (usually Nitrile) are standard for oil and fuel resistance. Silicone seals offer superior temperature range and weather resistance, making them better for outdoor components or high-heat engine environments in your 2026 projects.

Do I need special tools to install a hydraulic seal kit?

While some can be installed by hand, using seal picks and installation sleeves prevents damage to the new component. For mechanical restoration tools, a dedicated picking set is highly recommended to avoid gouging the metal grooves.

Are these kits suitable for modern 2026 electric tools?

Yes, even electric tools often have gearboxes or cooling systems that require waterproof components and gaskets to prevent grease leakage or moisture entry, making seal kits universally useful.

How long do seal kits last in storage?

When stored in a cool, dark, and dry place in their original packaging, high-quality seal kits can last 5-10 years. Keeping them away from UV light is critical to prevent the material from becoming brittle.
