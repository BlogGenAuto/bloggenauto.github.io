Frequently Asked Questions

Are wall mounts safe for nitrocellulose finishes in 2026?

Yes, but you must ensure the hooks use non-reactive materials. Our racks feature silicone sleeves which do not react with the delicate nitrocellulose finishes found on vintage and high-end instruments. We always recommend checking with our double-check instructions for peace of mind.

Can I use a wood guitar wall mount on a plasterboard wall?

It is possible, but you must use the correct wall anchors. For heavy loads, such as a 9-guitar rack, we recommend mounting into studs or using high-capacity toggle bolts. Because our rail system uses only 3 holes, you can often align them with structural supports more easily than individual hooks.

Does hanging a guitar by its neck cause damage?

No, guitars are designed to withstand significant tension from the strings (often over 100 lbs). The weight of the guitar itself (8-10 lbs) is negligible by comparison. Vertical storage is a standard practice in museums and high-end showrooms worldwide.

What is the benefit of the 'V' or 'Wave' mounting style?

Beyond being a visual statement, these styles allow you to stagger the bodies of the guitars. This is perfect for space saving guitar storage when you have a mix of thin electrics and deep-bodied acoustics that might otherwise overlap.

Will this work for a Fender Guitar Wall Mount setup with offset heads?

Absolutely. By simply rotating the hook, one prong sits higher than the other, perfectly cradling asymmetric headstocks. We also provide free safety straps to ensure these guitars stay seated securely.
