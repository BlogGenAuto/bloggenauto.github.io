FAQ: Common Questions About Horizontal Storage

Can you hang guitars horizontally?

Yes, hanging guitars horizontally or at an angle is a highly effective way to save vertical wall space and display the instrument body more prominently. It provides a secure cradle for the neck and body, often used in professional retail environments and high-end home studios to prevent the instrument from being bumped by foot traffic.

How to hang a guitar on the wall sideways?

To hang a guitar sideways, you need a specialized horizontal guitar wall mount or a rail system with adjustable hooks. You simply mount the rail, adjust the sliding hooks to the desired width for the neck and body, and rotate the hooks to achieve the perfect angle. This allows you to overlap instruments for maximum density.

Is it a good idea to hang a guitar on the wall?

Absolutely. Hanging a guitar on the wall keeps it away from pets, children, and vacuum cleaners that can cause accidental damage. It also encourages more frequent practice by keeping the instrument visible and accessible, and it helps maintain more consistent humidity levels compared to instruments kept on the floor.

Is it bad to lean a guitar against a wall?

Leaning a guitar against a wall is generally discouraged by luthiers because it puts direct pressure on the neck and headstock, which can lead to warping or structural damage over time. Additionally, guitars are highly unstable when leaned and can easily slide or fall, whereas a horizontal guitar wall mount provides a fixed, safe storage point.
