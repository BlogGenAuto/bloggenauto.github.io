Frequently Asked Questions

Is the guitar wall mount multiple safe for vintage nitrocellulose finishes?

Yes, the GearHooks GTR series uses high-quality silicone sleeves. Unlike standard rubber or cheap foam, silicone is non-reactive with the Nitrocellulose lacquer used on many vintage and high-end instruments. We always recommend checking our provided instructions for sensitive vintage gear.

How many guitars can I actually fit on a 1-meter rail?

Depending on the model (GTR3, GTR6, or GTR9), you can store up to 9 instruments on a single 1-meter rail. This is achieved by storing guitars at an angle, where one guitar sits slightly behind the other on a longer hook, maximizing every centimeter of wall space.

Can I store other instruments besides guitars?

Absolutely. The 45mm prong gap is ideal for guitars, but the system is modular. You can easily add specialized hooks for ukuleles (which can store up to 25 on one rail), mandolins, banjos, or even wind instruments like clarinets and cornets.

Is it difficult to install a guitar wall mount multiple rail myself?

Not at all. In fact, it is much easier than installing individual hooks. You only need to drill three 8mm holes for each rail. Because the rail uses a slotted mounting system, you have some wiggle room to ensure everything is perfectly level even if your drilling is slightly off.

What happens if I have an asymmetric headstock like a Telecaster?

The GearHooks hooks are rotatable. By rotating the hook slightly, one prong sits higher than the other, allowing instruments with asymmetric headstocks to sit securely. We also provide safety straps free of charge to ensure extra stability.

Does the rail come in different colors?

Yes, in 2026 we offer a range of colors including red, orange, blue, green, yellow, grey, white, and black. This allows you to match your studio decor or contrast with your guitar finishes for a custom look.
