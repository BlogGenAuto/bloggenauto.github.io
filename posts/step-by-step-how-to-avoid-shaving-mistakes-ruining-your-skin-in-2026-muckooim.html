Skipping Proper Cleansing: The #1 Men's Shaving Routine Error

One of the most common shaving mistakes is applying cream directly to a face that hasn't been washed. Dirt, excess sebum, and dead skin cells act as obstacles for your razor. When the blade hits these impurities, it jumps and tugs, causing uneven cuts and irritation.

Why Facial Cleansing is Non-Negotiable

A proper face wash before the blade touches the skin softens the hair follicles. This makes them easier to cut, requiring less pressure.
