Frequently Asked Questions

Are these guitar stands wall mount hooks safe for Nitrocellulose finishes?

Yes, our hooks use high-grade silicone sleeves. Unlike traditional rubber or foam, silicone does not react with nitrocellulose lacquer, making them safe for vintage and high-end 2026 instrument collections.

How many guitars can I fit on one rail?

Depending on the length of the hooks used (100mm, 200mm, or 300mm), you can store between 3 and 20 guitars on a single 1-meter rail by staggering them and using extra guitar hooks.

Can I use these hooks for heavy bass guitars?

Absolutely. The cold-formed steel construction is designed to support the weight of heavy solid-body bass guitars. We recommend ensuring the rail is securely mounted to wall studs for maximum stability.

Do I need special tools to install the guitar wall mount?

You will need a drill to mount the rail to the wall. Once the rail is up, the hooks can be added, moved, or adjusted using a simple Hex key, allowing for quick changes to your 2026 studio layout.

Will these hooks hold a 12-string guitar?

Most 12-string guitars fit the standard 45mm gap. For instruments with wider nuts, the steel prongs can be slightly spread manually to accommodate the wider neck while still maintaining structural integrity.
