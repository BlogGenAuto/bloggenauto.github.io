Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay to hang an acoustic guitar on the wall?

Yes, hanging an acoustic guitar on the wall is perfectly safe provided you use a high-quality acoustic guitar wall mount and ensure proper humidity control. In fact, it is often safer than a floor stand, as it prevents accidental knocks from pets, children, or vacuum cleaners. Just ensure the mount supports the neck properly and uses wall-appropriate fixings.

How safe are guitar wall mounts?

Modern wall mounts are incredibly safe, especially when using a system like GearHooks that employs a cold-rolled steel rail and heavy-duty bolts. When fixed into brick, block, or wooden studs, a modular rail can support up to 100kg, which is far beyond the weight of even the heaviest 2026 instrument collection. Always check that the hooks have a protective coating to prevent scratches on the instrument's finish.

What is the best guitar wall hanger in the UK?

The best guitar wall mount UK musicians can choose is one that offers both durability and flexibility. Is it better to hang or stand a guitar?

Hanging a guitar is generally superior for space management and safety. While floor stands are portable, they are prone to tipping and consume valuable floor space. Hanging your guitar on an acoustic guitar wall mount keeps the instrument at eye level, which encourages more frequent practice and protects the neck from unnecessary pressure often found in poorly designed floor stands.
