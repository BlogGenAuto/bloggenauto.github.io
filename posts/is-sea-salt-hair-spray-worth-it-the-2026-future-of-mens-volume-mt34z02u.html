Can I use sea salt spray on straight hair?

Yes, it is actually one of the best tools for straight hair. It provides the necessary friction and grit needed to create volume and waves that straight hair typically lacks. It transforms limp strands into a more structured, textured style.

How often should I apply volumizing hair spray?

You can use it daily as part of your styling routine. Because it is a lightweight, water-based formula, it does not cause the heavy buildup associated with waxes or clays. However, it is always recommended to wash your hair 2-3 times a week to maintain scalp health.

Is sea salt spray better than dry shampoo?

They serve different primary purposes. Dry shampoo is designed specifically to absorb oil. Sea salt spray provides volume and texture while also absorbing some oil due to the silica powder. If you want styling power plus oil control, sea salt spray is the superior all-in-one choice.
