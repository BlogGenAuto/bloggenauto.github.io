Frequently Asked Questions

Is it okay to hang guitars on a wall?

Yes, hanging guitars on a wall is a widely accepted and safe practice provided you use a high-quality guitar mount for wall. It keeps instruments away from floor-level hazards and allows for better air circulation, which helps maintain the wood's condition. Always ensure the rack is properly secured to a wall stud or uses appropriate heavy-duty fixings.

What is the best way to hang a guitar on the wall?

The best way to hang a guitar is using a system that supports the weight evenly and uses non-reactive materials on the contact points. A vertical guitar mount for wall that grips the headstock securely is ideal for saving space and providing easy access. Using a rail-based system is even better as it reduces the number of holes needed in your wall while providing superior weight capacity.

Is it better to hang or stand a guitar?

In most professional 2026 studio environments, hanging is considered superior for space management and safety. Floor stands are easily knocked over and consume valuable floor space that could be used for keyboards or pedals. A guitar mount for wall ensures the instrument is out of the "danger zone" while keeping it visible and ready to play.

Does a guitar wall mount need to be in a stud?

While it is always preferable to mount into a wall stud for maximum security, modern high-capacity rails and industrial-strength wall anchors allow for secure mounting on various wall types. If you are mounting a multi-instrument rack designed to hold 9 guitars, securing at least one part of the rail into a stud is highly recommended due to the cumulative weight.
