Frequently Asked Questions

Can you hang a bass guitar on a wall?

Yes, hanging a bass guitar on a wall is a standard practice in professional studios. However, because bass guitars are significantly heavier than standard electric guitars, it is crucial to use a high-capacity bass guitar wall mount that is securely fixed to studs or masonry. A rail-based system like GearHooks distributes the weight across multiple fixing points, providing extra peace of mind for heavy vintage basses.

Can you hang a bass on the wall?

Absolutely, and it is often preferred over floor stands to prevent accidental tip-overs. When you hang a bass on the wall, you ensure that the neck is supported and the body is clear of floor-level hazards. Using modular wall rails allows you to adjust the spacing between instruments so that large-bodied basses don't touch each other.

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Wall mounts are perfectly safe for guitars as long as they are installed correctly and use high-quality materials. Professional systems use powder coated storage hooks with protective padding to ensure the finish of your instrument is never compromised. Many luthiers actually prefer wall mounting because it keeps instruments away from the pets, children, and cleaning equipment found at floor level.

How to store a bass guitar without a stand?

If you don't want to use a traditional tripod stand, the best alternative is a wall-mounted gear rack. This method keeps the instrument upright and secure while freeing up floor space. For those who want a diy bass guitar wall mount, a modular rail system is the easiest way to store multiple instruments without having to measure and drill separate holes for every single guitar hook.
