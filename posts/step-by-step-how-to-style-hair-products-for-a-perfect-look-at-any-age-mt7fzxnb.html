Will strong hold pomade make my hair fall out?

No, as long as you use high-quality, water-soluble products. Traditional waxes with petroleum can clog follicles, but plant-based pomades with ingredients like Platycladus actually help fortify the hair. Always ensure you wash your hair regularly to prevent any product buildup.

How do I style hair styling products for thinning hair?

For thinning hair, use a lightweight strong hold pomade on dry or slightly damp hair. Avoid heavy gels. Apply a small amount primarily to the tips of the hair to create volume without weighing down the roots, which can make thinning areas more visible.

What is the difference between water-soluble pomade and traditional wax?

Water-soluble pomades are made with a water base, meaning they rinse out easily and offer a cleaner finish. Traditional waxes are often oil-based, providing a "greasier" look and requiring multiple shampoos to remove, which can strip hair of its natural moisture.

Does pomade cause dandruff?

Pomade itself doesn't cause dandruff, but "product buildup" from poor-quality waxes can mimic flaking. Using a water-soluble formula ensures no residue is left behind, keeping the scalp clean and reducing the risk of irritation-related flaking.
