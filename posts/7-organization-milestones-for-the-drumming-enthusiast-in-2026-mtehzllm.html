Frequently Asked Questions

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Yes, wall mounts are highly recommended for guitars as they keep instruments away from pets, children, and accidental kicks on the floor. When using a professional-grade multiple guitar wall mount, the weight is distributed evenly across a steel rail, ensuring your instruments remain stable and secure while also being showcased as art.

How to display multiple guitars?

The most efficient way to display multiple guitars is by using a modular rail system. This allows you to adjust the spacing between each instrument, accommodating different body shapes like acoustics and electrics. A multiple guitar wall mount setup ensures that your collection remains accessible for practice while significantly enhancing the visual appeal of your studio.

Is it better to hang a guitar or keep it in a case?

While cases offer maximum protection from humidity, hanging a guitar is often better for active musicians because it encourages more frequent practice. By using a secure multiple guitar wall mount, you maintain the structural integrity of the instrument's neck while ensuring it is always ready to play. For long-term storage, cases are fine, but for a working 2026 studio, wall mounting is the superior choice.
