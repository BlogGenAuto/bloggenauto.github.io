Are these hooks safe for vintage nitrocellulose finishes?

Yes. Extra Guitar Hooks are fitted with inert medical-grade silicone sleeves that do not leach oils or plasticizers. Unlike cheap synthetic rubber or low-grade foam padding, silicone will not chemically react with, stain, or soften delicate nitrocellulose lacquer.

Can I fit a guitar wall mount white system onto stud walls and masonry?

GearRails and individual hooks attach securely to masonry, brick, or timber stud partitions using standard heavy-duty architectural fixings. Once mounted, the rail distributes the weight evenly across multiple anchor points for complete peace of mind.

How do multi-guitar hooks prevent neck collision?

The DP45/200S (2 guitars) and DP45/300S (3 guitars) feature tiered spacing and protective safety straps. This prevents instruments hung front-to-back from touching, allowing acoustic, electric, and bass models to hang safely on a single hook axis.

What makes cold-formed steel hooks more sustainable than floor stands?

Cold-formed steel uses minimal heat processing, maintains immense structural load-bearing capacity, and resists fatigue indefinitely. Floor stands break at welded or plastic joints, whereas steel rail hardware can be disassembled, adjusted, and fully recycled decades later.

Can I adjust the prong width for classical or 12-string guitars?

The standard 45mm prong gap is calibrated for electric, bass, and standard acoustic guitars. Because the prongs are manufactured from robust cold-formed steel, they can be manually widened to cradle wide-neck classical, Spanish, or 12-string instruments without structural failure.

Can extra guitar hooks fit other gear besides instruments?

The sliding CNC clamp system accepts accessories between guitar necks. Musicians frequently use intermediate rail space to store heavy instrument cables, audio snakes, headphones, or gig bags on dedicated utility attachments.
