FAQ

What makes water-soluble pomades better for the scalp?

Water-soluble pomades rinse out easily, preventing residue buildup that can block pores and cause irritation, thus promoting better scalp health.

Can the sandalwood fragrance in pomades affect sensitive skin?

No, the sandalwood fragrance is typically gentle and should not affect sensitive skin negatively. However, it is always advisable to conduct a patch test first.

How often should I wash my hair if I use pomade daily?

It's best to wash your hair every other day to prevent drying out the scalp while still removing product buildup effectively.

Are plant-based hair care products effective for all hair types?

Yes, plant-based products are generally suitable for all hair types as they nourish without harsh chemicals, making them a versatile option.

What other natural ingredients should I look for in hair styling products?

Look for ingredients like jojoba oil, aloe vera, and tea tree oil, which provide moisture and have anti-inflammatory properties to support scalp health.
