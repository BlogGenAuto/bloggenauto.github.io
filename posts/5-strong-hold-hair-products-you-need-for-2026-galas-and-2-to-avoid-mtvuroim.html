How do I stop my hair from flaking at a long event?

Avoid alcohol-based gels. Instead, use a water-based styling pomade. These formulas remain flexible and do not dry out into white dust, even after 12 hours of wear.

Can I use this pomade on thick, curly hair?

Absolutely. Pomade for thick hair men needs to have high tension to tame unruly curls. Apply the product when hair is slightly damp for maximum control and a sharp, defined finish.

What is the difference between a natural shine and a greasy finish?

A natural shine reflects light evenly, making hair look healthy. A greasy finish looks "wet" and separates the hair into oily strands, which is usually caused by excessive petroleum or silicone in the product.

Sandalwood is a base note in many premium men's fragrances. Using sandalwood fragrance hair products typically provides a warm, subtle undertone that enhances your existing scent profile rather than fighting it.
