Application Mastery for Overnight Smoothness

To maximize the benefits of your shave stick, follow these steps to ensure your skin is perfectly prepped:

  2. Apply: Twist up the stick and rub it directly against the grain of your beard growth.
  3. Lather: Use your fingertips or a brush to create a creamy lather on the skin.
