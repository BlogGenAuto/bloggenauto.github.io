Frequently Asked Questions

Is matte clay pomade better than hair wax?

Yes, especially if you prefer a natural look. While wax often leaves a shine and can be greasy, matte clay provides a shine-free finish and more texture, making it superior for modern, messy, or professional styles in 2026.

How do I apply clay pomade for the best hold?

Start with a small, pea-sized amount. Rub it vigorously between your palms to warm up the beeswax and clay. Apply to towel-dried hair, starting from the back and moving to the front to ensure even distribution without clumping.

Does it work on thin or fine hair?

Absolutely. In fact, kaolin clay is one of the best ingredients for fine hair because it adds physical girth to each strand and provides lift at the base, creating the illusion of much thicker hair.
