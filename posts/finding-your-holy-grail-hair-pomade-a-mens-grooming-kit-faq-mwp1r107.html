What does heavy hold pomade do?

A heavy hold pomade provides maximum control and structural reinforcement for complex hairstyles like high pompadours, tight slick-backs, and structured side parts. Why did people stop using pomade?

Many men stepped away from vintage pomades because traditional petroleum-based formulas were notoriously difficult to wash out, often requiring harsh dish soaps or special degreasers. Furthermore, these dense mineral oils frequently clogged forehead pores, causing pomade acne. Modern water-soluble pomades have revived the category by rinsing clean in seconds with water alone.

Among modern styling options, dedicated high-hold water-based pomades and concentrated styling clays provide the highest degree of structural endurance.
