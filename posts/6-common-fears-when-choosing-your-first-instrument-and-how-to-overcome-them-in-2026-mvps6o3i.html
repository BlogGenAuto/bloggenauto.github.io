Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best way to store a bass guitar?

The best way to store a bass guitar is using a dedicated bass guitar wall mount that supports the instrument at its strongest point—the neck and headstock. This keeps the instrument off the floor, away from humidity changes found near the ground, and safe from being knocked over. High-quality wall racks also allow for air circulation around the body, which is beneficial for the wood’s longevity.

What is the holy grail of bass guitars?

While subjective, many consider the 1951 Fender Precision Bass or the 1960s Fender Jazz Bass to be the holy grails of the industry. These instruments represent the birth of the modern electric bass and are often valued at tens of thousands of pounds. For such high-value items, using a heavy-duty bass guitar wall mount with nitrocellulose-safe silicone is mandatory for preservation.

Is it okay to lean a guitar against a wall?

No, leaning a guitar against a wall is generally discouraged by experts at Yamaha. This position puts improper pressure on the neck and headstock, potentially leading to warping or neck breaks if the instrument slips. It also leaves the body vulnerable to impacts from passersby or pets.

Are wall mounts ok for guitars?

Wall mounts are widely considered one of the safest storage methods for guitars when installed correctly into a solid wall or stud. They remove the risk of the instrument being tripped over and keep them in a consistent environment. Using a bass guitar wall mount system that uses a rail further increases safety by distributing the weight across multiple points.
